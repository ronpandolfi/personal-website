Ronald J. Pandolfi

I build control systems and scientific software for synchrotron beamlines at the Advanced Light Source: control UIs, autonomous experiment orchestration, and the infrastructure that keeps instruments and their software reproducible.

Role

CAMERA · AMCR
Computer Systems Engineer in CAMERA, the Center for Advanced Mathematics for Energy Research Applications, part of Berkeley Lab's Applied Mathematics and Computational Research Division. I turn applied-math methods into production software for DOE user facilities.
ASCRIBE
Co-PI of ASCRIBE, an extended-reality scientific visualization project. ASCRIBE-XR brings scientific imagery (meshes, volumes, point clouds) into virtual reality, and the ASCRIBE-Link service streams it to headsets live from running experiments.
ALS BEAMLINE CONTROLS · BCG
I work with the ALS Beamline Controls Group on the facility-wide move to EPICS, building the software that carries the transition: configuration management (CSM), provisioning automation, and IOC telemetry (IOCular). Lightfall, the next-generation control dashboard, is in commissioning at COSMIC-Scattering.
